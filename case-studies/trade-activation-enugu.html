Case note · Execution

Trade activation through repeatable field routines.

A practical example of connecting commercial priorities to outlet-level execution. Internal rankings and scorecards are not presented here as public proof.

The problem

Trade activity can become a one-day push if priorities are not translated into repeatable actions at outlet level.

What I worked on

Connected activation priorities to field planning, outlet execution, visibility, availability and follow-up across the market.

What changed

The work supported a more repeatable execution rhythm. Internal ranking claims and confidential commercial scorecards are not published as verified outcomes.

Method

Turn activation into a routine, not a moment.

01TranslateTurn priorities into field actions.
02PlanSet outlet and route focus.
03ExecuteWork on availability and visibility.
04ReviewCheck execution against the plan.
05Follow upClose gaps after the visit.
06RepeatBuild a consistent field rhythm.

What I learned

Activation becomes more useful when the field team can repeat the behaviour after the launch moment has passed.
