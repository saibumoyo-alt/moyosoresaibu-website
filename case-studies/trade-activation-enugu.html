Case study · Trade activation

Trade activation built around repeatable execution

Activation is only useful if it changes what happens in the outlet after the event moment ends.

Result#1 Area, Division and Regional status — publicly shared, internal scorecard not published.
MarketEnugu, Nigeria
EvidencePublic professional record with stated limits

The situation

The Enugu chapter required consistent execution across multiple priority territories during a period publicly described as a business transition. The commercial need was to keep outlet-level activity steady while everything around it was changing.

What I did

  • Connected brand priorities to outlet-level availability, visibility and repeatable routines.
  • Used route-to-market thinking to make activation operational, not decorative.
  • Prioritized outlets by real opportunity instead of by convenience or habit.

The result

The public professional record states #1 status at Area, Division and Regional levels during this chapter. Internal scorecard details, weighting and ranking window are not published.

What I learned

Activation only compounds when the routine survives after the event moment ends. A single strong week does not fix outlet-level execution on its own — the repeatable part is what carries the result.

Evidence limitThe #1 status is a publicly shared professional outcome. The internal scorecard, weighting and ranking window are not public, so this page does not claim an independent audit.
