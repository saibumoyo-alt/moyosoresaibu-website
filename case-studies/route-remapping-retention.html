Case study · Route planning

Customer retention through route remapping

A simple example of how better route focus and customer follow-up supported a stronger retention result.

Result+22% customer retention improvement — publicly shared from the Enugu chapter and attributed to data-led route efficiency and remapping.
MarketEnugu, Nigeria
EvidencePublic professional record with stated limits

The situation

The Enugu trade activation role covered multiple priority markets. The commercial need was to use field time better, improve visit quality and make customer follow-up more consistent instead of treating every call the same.

What I did

  • Used customer and route information to improve route efficiency and remap coverage.
  • Focused visits around customer priority and buying rhythm.
  • Used a clearer follow-up rhythm so the next action did not disappear after the visit.
  • Reviewed the route as a commercial tool, not only as a travel plan.

The result

+22% customer retention improvement — publicly shared from the Enugu chapter and attributed to data-led route efficiency and remapping.

What I learned

Route planning works best when it improves the quality and timing of customer contact. More movement is not the same as better coverage.

Evidence limitThe +22% figure is a publicly shared professional outcome. The internal baseline, raw customer cohort, detailed workbook and company scorecard are not public, so this page does not claim an independent audit.
