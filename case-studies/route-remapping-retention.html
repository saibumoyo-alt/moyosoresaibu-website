Route Remapping & Customer Retention

A route is not a calendar. It is an allocation of commercial attention.

Route-to-market
A route is not a calendar. It is an allocation of commercial attention.
+22% retentionselected public outcome

Situation

In the Enugu chapter, execution covered markets including 9th Mile, Uwani, Ogui, New Haven and Independence Layout. The challenge was to make field attention more deliberate and repeatable.

Action

Re-map execution around customer location and priority; reduce unnecessary movement; make follow-up easier to repeat; use route efficiency as a customer-retention lever.

Result

A +22% customer-retention increase was publicly shared and attributed to data-driven route efficiency. The site does not publish the internal baseline or raw customer file.
