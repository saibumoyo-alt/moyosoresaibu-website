Case note · Routes

Route remapping and customer follow-up.

A practical example of using route priorities and follow-up discipline to focus field effort. Confidential commercial performance data is not published as proof.

The problem

Field effort can drift when route priorities, customer importance and follow-up are not aligned.

What I worked on

Reviewed route logic, customer priority and visit rhythm, then used available data as decision support for where field time should go.

What changed

The work informed a more structured route and follow-up approach. This page does not publish an independently verified retention uplift.

Method

Keep route decisions connected to customer reality.

01ReviewLook at route and customer context.
02PrioritiseDecide where time matters most.
03PlanSet a workable visit rhythm.
04Follow upClose the loop after the visit.
05MeasureTrack what changed internally.
06AdjustRefine the route as conditions change.

What I learned

Route design is useful only when it changes field priorities and improves the quality of follow-up. A route map on its own is not a result.
