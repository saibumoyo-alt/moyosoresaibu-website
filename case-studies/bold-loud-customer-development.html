Case note · Customers

Customer development at outlet level.

A practical example of working on availability, visibility and consistent customer follow-up. The site does not present an unsupported outlet ranking as proof.

The problem

Outlet execution can weaken when product availability, visibility and relationship follow-up are treated as separate tasks.

What I worked on

Used regular customer conversations and field follow-up to understand needs, address execution gaps and keep commercial priorities visible.

What changed

The work supported a more deliberate customer-development routine. Customer-specific commercial data and unsupported ranking claims are not published here.

Method

Build the outlet relationship around useful execution.

01ListenUnderstand the outlet need.
02CheckReview availability and visibility.
03PrioritiseFocus on the biggest friction.
04ActAddress the agreed issue.
05Follow upReturn to what was promised.
06LearnUse feedback in the next visit.

What I learned

Customer development is not a badge or ranking. It is the repeated work of understanding the outlet, reducing friction and following through.
