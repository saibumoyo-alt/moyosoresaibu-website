Cultural Activation: Enugu Abacha Festival

Cultural relevance can turn sponsorship into a market experience people actually remember.

Brand activation
Cultural relevance can turn sponsorship into a market experience people actually remember.
6,000+ attendeesselected public outcome

Situation

The Enugu Abacha Festival brought Orijin RTS and Dubic Malt into a culturally resonant event environment.

Action

Use product presence, cultural context and consumer experience together rather than treating brand visibility as a standalone asset.

Result

The public recap states more than 6,000 attendees. The figure is presented as a public event-scale claim, not an independently audited footfall study.
