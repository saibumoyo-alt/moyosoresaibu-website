# Customer Retention Sources — 8.0

Sources checked on 16 August 2026.

## Service and repeat purchase
- Salesforce — Customer Expectations
  https://www.salesforce.com/research/customer-expectations/
  Useful current signal: 88% say good customer service makes them more likely to purchase again; 56% often need to repeat information to different representatives.

## Empathy
- Zurich Insurance + YouGov — Addressing the Empathy Gap (2025)
  https://www.zurich.com/media/news-releases/2025/2025-0923-01
  Useful current signal: 73% would avoid businesses that show a lack of empathy.

## Service recovery
- Journal of Brand Management — From service failure to brand loyalty: evidence of service recovery paradox (2025)
  https://link.springer.com/article/10.1057/s41262-025-00380-5
  Useful conclusion: satisfactory recovery can rebuild brand engagement and loyalty; the website does not claim a universal fixed uplift percentage.

## Personalization
- McKinsey — The value of getting personalization right—or wrong—is multiplying
  https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/our-insights/the-value-of-getting-personalization-right-or-wrong-is-multiplying
  Useful signal: 78% said personalized content made them more likely to repurchase.

## Involuntary churn
- Stripe Billing — Revenue recovery
  https://stripe.com/gb/billing/features
  Useful current signals: nearly a quarter of churn is involuntary; Smart Retries recover 57% of recurring payments that originally failed on average.

## Loyalty programs
- Deloitte — Reshaping customer loyalty programs (2025 survey)
  https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/industry/retail-distribution/reshaping-customer-loyalty-programs.html
  Useful current signals: 72% say loyalty programs make them more likely to spend with their preferred brand; 56% increase spending because of the program.

## Social customer service
- McKinsey — Social media as a service differentiator
  https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/operations/our-insights/social-media-as-a-service-differentiator-how-to-win
  Useful signal: customers spend 20–40% more with companies that respond to service requests on social media; non-response is associated with higher churn.

## Research hygiene
The 8.0 website intentionally avoids repeating unsupported or weakly sourced viral statistics such as a universal 18% service-recovery retention uplift, 26% 12-month retention uplift from recommendations, or 8.6% first-year revenue recovery unless a primary source can be verified.
