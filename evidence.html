Evidence & context

What this site can support — and what it does not claim.

This site does not use self-published performance metrics as independent proof. Where underlying scorecards, internal baselines or raw commercial data are not public, those figures are not presented as verified outcomes.

Not published as proof

What is deliberately excluded.

Some commercial information may exist in professional records but is not suitable for a public credibility claim without independently accessible support.

Internal scorecards

Confidential ranking, target and performance records are not treated as public verification.

Customer-level data

Commercially sensitive outlet, distributor and customer data is not published.

Self-reported outcomes

Numbers without accessible underlying evidence are not used here as proof of performance.

Trust standard

How claims are handled.

01SeparatePublic record from self-report.
02QualifyState context and limits.
03SourceLink external research where used.
04AvoidUnsupported rankings and superlatives.
05ProtectKeep confidential commercial data private.
06CorrectRemove claims that cannot be supported.

Professional experience is useful context. It is not the same as independent verification.

That distinction is intentional across this website.
