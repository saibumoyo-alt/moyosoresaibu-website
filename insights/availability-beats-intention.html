Insight by Moyosore Saibu

Availability Beats Intention in the Market

Moyosore Saibu explains why availability is one of the most practical foundations of commercial execution.

A customer cannot buy what is not available. That sounds obvious, but it is one of the easiest commercial truths to overlook when teams become distracted by activity instead of outcomes.

Great execution starts with the basics: the right product, in the right outlet, at the right time, in a condition the customer can actually purchase.

Visibility can create desire. Communication can build awareness. But availability is where commercial intent becomes a transaction.

— Moyosore Saibu
