Route Discipline Is a Competitive Advantage

Why route discipline, customer prioritisation and consistent coverage matter in field sales execution.

A route plan is more than a schedule. It is a decision about where time, energy and attention will create the most value.

Good route discipline reduces unnecessary movement, improves customer coverage and creates more room for quality conversations. It also makes performance easier to diagnose because execution becomes consistent enough to measure.

The advantage is not simply visiting more customers. It is visiting the right customers with a clear purpose, then following through.

— Moyosore Saibu
