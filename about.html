About Moyosore Saibu

Field discipline. Commercial curiosity. Measurable value.

Moyosore Saibu is a Nigerian commercial professional whose work spans sales execution, trade activation, territory management, customer development and digital communication.

Professional profile

A field-first mindset.

My work is grounded in a simple idea: commercial performance improves when strategy is translated into clear actions at customer and territory level.

I have built experience across field sales, trade activation and territory management — working with route planning, customer coverage, availability, visibility, retention, execution quality, data and communication.

What matters most to me is useful execution: understanding the commercial problem, designing a practical response and following through until the market result changes.
