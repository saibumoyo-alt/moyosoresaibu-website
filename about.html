About me

I am a sales and commercial professional in Nigeria. I currently work as a Territory Manager at Guinness Nigeria.

My work is about making plans useful in the real market.

I started in field sales, moved into trade activation, and now manage territory performance. That path has taught me how much sales depends on customer understanding, clear priorities and consistent follow-up.

I work with route planning, customer coverage, availability, visibility, sales reviews and simple data. I also use digital tools and AI when they can remove repetitive work or make a decision easier.

How I work

Keep the problem clear.

I use a simple three-part approach.

Understand the problem

Look at the customer, the route and the numbers before choosing an answer.

Take a practical action

Choose something the team can understand, own and repeat.

Review the result

Check what changed. Keep what worked and fix what did not.
