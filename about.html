About

I work on problems where people, execution and growth stop lining up.

My job title can change. The pattern I work on stays the same: understand the friction, focus the useful move and make execution clearer.

I am most useful when the problem is real and the answer is not obvious.

I have built my commercial experience through field sales, trade activation and territory responsibility. That work taught me that many growth problems are not caused by a lack of effort; they come from unclear priorities, weak customer context, broken follow-up or data that never becomes a decision.

I combine field observation, customer understanding, route and performance data, simple systems and AI-assisted workflows when they genuinely reduce friction. The goal is not more complexity. It is a clearer next move.

How I think

Start with the constraint, not the activity.

I use a practical three-question lens.

What is actually blocking progress?

Look at the customer, route, behaviour and numbers before assuming the answer.

What move creates the most leverage?

Choose a clear action a person or team can understand, own and repeat.

What changed after the move?

Review the result, keep the lesson and improve the system instead of protecting the idea.
