FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about what I help with, how to start, evidence, privacy and how the site works.

Tell me what’s stuck.

Before you get in touch

What kinds of problems can I bring?

Sales conversion, customer retention, route-to-market, field execution, commercial prioritisation and practical growth problems are the clearest fit. Strategy, digital and AI can support the solution when they are relevant.

Who is the site for?

It is built for businesses, sales teams, distributors, growing brands and people responsible for turning a commercial plan into action.

Do I need a perfect brief before contacting you?

No. Start with three things: the problem, the goal and the blocker. That is enough to begin a useful conversation.

How does an engagement start?

Use the contact form, WhatsApp or Telegram to describe what is stuck. The first step is to understand the problem before recommending a solution or scope.

How quickly should I expect a reply?

When a response is needed, the site’s contact flow states an aim to reply within two working days. Actual timing can vary with the request and availability.

How are performance claims handled?

The site links key figures to available source evidence and states important limits on what those figures prove. Published case-study results should be read in their original context.

What information does this site store on my device?

Browser local storage may keep language, optional first-name personalisation, recently selected categories and your privacy choice. These preferences are designed to stay on the device unless a feature explicitly needs otherwise.

Does the site use analytics or cookies?

The site uses local storage for essential preferences. Optional first-party page and CTA measurement is sent only after you choose “Allow analytics,” respects Do Not Track, and is designed not to include contact-message text, names or email addresses.

Can I contact you without using the form?

Yes. WhatsApp and Telegram links are available throughout the site, and the contact page provides the available direct channels.

Does the site work on mobile and with accessibility settings?

The site is designed responsively for mobile and desktop, includes keyboard-accessible navigation, a skip link, labelled controls and support for reduced-motion preferences. Accessibility is treated as an ongoing quality requirement.
