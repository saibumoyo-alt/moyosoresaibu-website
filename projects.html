Solutions

Start with the problem. Then look at the evidence.

I am most useful on commercial problems where people, routes, priorities or information are getting in the way of growth.

Problem map

Four kinds of friction I can help untangle.

Each solution starts with diagnosis. The examples below describe the problem pattern, not a one-size-fits-all package.

Sales execution

The plan is not becoming action.

Look for: unclear priorities, inconsistent follow-up, weak visit quality or activity without movement.

I help by: translating targets into customer priorities, ownership and repeatable next actions.

Customer growth

Customers are not staying, buying or growing as expected.

Look for: retention gaps, weak availability, low visibility, poor experience or relationship friction.

I help by: finding the customer-side constraint and improving the execution around it.

Route-to-market

Time and effort are going to the wrong places.

Look for: inefficient routes, weak coverage, low-priority calls or unclear outlet focus.

I help by: using route, customer and performance signals to focus effort where it can matter most.

Decision systems

Data is describing the past instead of improving the next move.

Look for: reports without decisions, repetitive admin or information scattered across tools.

I help by: simplifying the signal with dashboards, practical reviews and AI-assisted workflows where useful.
