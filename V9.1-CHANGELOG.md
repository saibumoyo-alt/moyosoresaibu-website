# V9.1 — Additive Transformation

Nothing useful from V8.2/V9 was removed. V9.1 adds a stronger experience layer on top of the existing SEO, evidence, performance, contact, insights and conversion architecture.

## Homepage
- Cinematic first-load reveal without a framework.
- FIELD × SYSTEM identity signal added to the hero.
- New three-part operating line and fast visitor routes.
- New full-width kinetic manifesto: READ THE SIGNAL → CHOOSE THE CONSTRAINT → MOVE THE MARKET.
- Subtle portrait/title depth on fine-pointer devices.
- Chapter rail and section-aware progress.
- Sequential method emphasis and restrained microinteractions.
- Custom response cursor only on fine-pointer devices; disabled on touch/reduced motion.

## /start/
- MEET → CHOOSE → MOVE badge and kinetic opening line.
- Five doors now preview their destination before the user clicks.
- People lens is interactive: Customers / Work / Money / Life explain the human principle behind each path.
- Story rail tracks progression through Choose / Current / Proof / People / Move.
- Cinematic load/depth treatment while preserving the warm editorial identity.
- Existing Current ↔ Evergreen, proof, latest thinking, tools, and CTAs remain intact.

## Design principles compounded
- Leedlime: direct promise, proof close to promise, short journey, freshness, clear next action.
- Digital Original XR: experiential mode switching, progressive journey, presence rather than flat explanation.
- Hue & Code: duality as positioning, proof/process clarity, human ownership, performance/accessibility promises.
- Moyosore: field-first commercial judgment, people lens, real evidence, current + evergreen thinking, hand-made editorial detail, AI/system discipline.
