Privacy

Simple, transparent data handling.

What the site collects, why it is used, and what choices visitors have.

What this site collects

The site may collect information you choose to submit through the contact form or Field Notes signup, such as your name, email address, organisation and message. That information is used only to respond to your enquiry or record your signup request.

Analytics

The preferred analytics setup is Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate page and performance measurement. Custom CTA analytics, when enabled, record event name and page context without intentionally storing your message content or email address.

Audio

Ambient audio is off by default. It only starts after you choose the Sound control. Your preference may be remembered for the current browsing session.

Contact data

Form submissions are sent to the site owner through a dedicated Cloudflare Worker using Cloudflare Email Service. The site does not intentionally place the message body or email address into conversion analytics. A direct email fallback remains available if secure form delivery is unavailable.

Your choice

You can contact saibumoyo@gmail.com to ask about information you submitted through this site.
