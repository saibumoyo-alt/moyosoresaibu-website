Privacy

Simple privacy information

This page explains what the website may collect and how it is used.

Information you send

If you use the contact form or join Field Notes, the site receives the information you choose to send, such as your name, email address and message.

Contact form delivery

The form is sent through a Cloudflare Worker connected to the website. If the form is unavailable, you can email directly instead.

If you join Field Notes, your email address is sent through the same Cloudflare Worker used by the contact form so the signup request can reach me. You can ask to be removed at any time by email.

Field Notes email signup

Analytics

The site may use privacy-focused website analytics to understand general page traffic and performance. Your contact-message text is not intentionally used for website analytics.

Your choice

You can email saibumoyo@gmail.com if you have a question about information you submitted through this website.
