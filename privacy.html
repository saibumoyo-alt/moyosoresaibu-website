Privacy

Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal information may be collected through this website and WhatsApp, why it is used and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

When you use the contact form, join an email list or otherwise contact me through this website, I may receive information you choose to provide. This may include your name, email address, organisation, the subject of your enquiry and the contents of your message.

If you contact me through WhatsApp, I may receive your WhatsApp profile name, phone number or WhatsApp account identifier, message content, attachments you choose to send, and technical message information such as the date, time and delivery status. WhatsApp and Meta may process this information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Website and service information

The site and its service providers may process limited technical information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as request times, browser or device information, approximate location derived from an IP address, and security or error records. The site may also use privacy-focused analytics to understand general traffic and performance. Contact-message text is not intentionally used for website analytics.

Why information is used

Personal information may be used to receive and respond to enquiries, continue conversations you start, provide requested professional information or services, manage email signups, maintain relevant business records, understand and improve the website, and detect or address misuse, fraud, security incidents or technical problems.

WhatsApp information is used to receive and manage the conversation you initiate. It is not used to send unrelated marketing messages without an appropriate reason or permission.

Sharing and service providers

I do not sell personal information. Limited information may be processed by service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the website, deliver forms or email, provide hosting and security, measure site performance, or support WhatsApp communications. These providers may include website infrastructure, email delivery, analytics, and Meta or WhatsApp services. They process information under their own contractual and privacy obligations.

Information may also be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to a valid legal request, protect rights or safety, or investigate misuse. It is not shared more broadly for unrelated purposes.

Retention

Personal information is kept only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, including responding to an enquiry, maintaining an ongoing professional relationship, keeping necessary business records, resolving disputes, meeting legal obligations, or protecting the service. Retention periods may vary with the nature and context of the interaction. Information that is no longer reasonably needed is deleted or anonymised where practical.

Your requests

You may ask whether I hold personal information about you, request a copy or correction, or ask for deletion. Use the existing contact page and describe your request. I may need to confirm enough information to identify the relevant records and may retain information where reasonably required for legal, security or record-keeping purposes.

Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to reduce the risk of unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. No website, messaging platform, transmission method or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please avoid sending information that is not needed for your enquiry.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, communication channels or relevant practices change. The effective date below will be revised when a material update is published.

Effective date: 22 August 2026.
